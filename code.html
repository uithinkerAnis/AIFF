THE FESTIVAL • CHHATRAPATI SAMBHAJI NAGAR (AURANGABAD)

Ajanta Ellora International
Film Festival

he festival aims to showcase the best of film produced recently and in the past, in India and other parts of the world, to the city of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and its film buffs. This festival is a platform for aspiring filmmakers, technicians, artists and young film enthusiasts to encourage them to learn and understand artistic as well as technical aspects of cinema. This festival also aims to be a medium to project Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Previously known as Aurangabad) and Marathwada internationally as the city and region for many opportunities of film production hub, cultural centre in state of Maharashtra.

auto_awesome CINEMA & CULTURAL EXCHANGE

AIFF brings acclaimed films from India and around the world to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, creating a space where filmmakers, artists, technicians and film lovers come together to share ideas and explore the craft of cinema.

palette MARATHWADA'S GLOBAL STAGE

Rooted in the cultural heritage of Marathwada, AIFF aims to establish Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar as a global cultural centre and film production hub while showcasing contemporary Marathi cinema to audiences across the world.

The Festival

A platform for cinema
in the land of caves

The Ajanta Ellora International Film Festival aims to showcase the best of film produced recently and in the past, in India and other parts of the world, to the city of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and its film buffs.

This festival is a platform for aspiring filmmakers, technicians, artists and young film enthusiasts to encourage them to learn and understand artistic as well as technical aspects of cinema.

It also aims to project Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and Marathwada internationally as a film production hub and cultural centre in the state of Maharashtra.

Heritage

Born from the spirit of Ajanta & Ellora

Just as the caves preserve timeless stories in stone and pigment, AIFF preserves and celebrates the art of storytelling on screen — connecting ancient wisdom with contemporary cinema.

Experience

Workshops • Seminars • Masterclasses

Along with a feast of films from around the world, the festival hosts Film Appreciation Workshops, Special Screenings, Seminars & Masterclasses that open doors for the next generation of storytellers.

Vision

Building a film production hub

AIFF positions Marathwada and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar as an emerging destination for film production, cultural exchange, and international collaboration.

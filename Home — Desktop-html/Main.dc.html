ABOUT AIFF

World cinema, in the city of Ajanta & Ellora

The festival brings the best of recent and classic cinema from India and around the world to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and its film lovers. It is a platform for aspiring filmmakers, technicians, artists and young enthusiasts to learn the art and craft of cinema — and to put Marathwada on the map as a hub for film production and culture.

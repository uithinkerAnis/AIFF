09 / THE MONOLITHIC CRADLE

WHERE CINEMA
MEETS CULTURE.

A FESTIVAL ROOTED IN HISTORY. A CINEMA LOOKING TOWARD THE FUTURE.

Carved over a millennium from volcanic basalt cliff walls, the world-renowned Ajanta and Ellora caves stand as humanity's most breathtaking testament to visual storytelling, volumetric light, and narrative sculpture.

AIFF continues this two-thousand-year heritage by hosting contemporary world cinema on this ancient sacred soil—where monks and sculptors first mastered the framing of light in dark voids.

DELEGATE HERITAGE EXCURSIONS COMPLIMENTARY

Daily air-conditioned express shuttles connect the festival screening hub with the Ajanta Caves (Caves 1–26) and Ellora Kailash complex with resident archaeological historians.
